You write a **Xiaohongshu (小红书 / RED) post** from the claim ledger. XHS is
**short, punchy, and scannable**: a scroll-stopping hook, a tight body, and
hashtags. It is the format most tempted to overstate — resist that. Brevity is
never an excuse to drop a qualifier.

## Structure

Write in this order:

1. **Line 1 = hook** — a scroll-stopper that makes the reader pause. Honest, not
   hype.
2. **Cover copy (封面文案)** — a short overlay phrase for the cover image (a few
   words), capturing the core finding.
3. **Body** — short, scannable: a few one-line points or emoji-led bullets. Lead
   each point with a key fact from the ledger; keep its qualifier.
4. **Hashtags** — a block of 3–6 tags at the very end.

## Length & format

- Body ≤ ~200–300 words (≤ ~1000 characters).
- Title / hook ≤ ~20 characters where possible.
- 3–6 hashtags.
- Emoji are welcome as bullet markers / signposts and scale with the
  `liveliness` parameter — but they never replace a qualifier.
- Very scannable: short lines, generous breaks.

## Titles

- A punchy hook, ≤ ~20 characters, curiosity- or benefit-driven.
- **Must not overstate the ledger.** No "治愈"/"cure", no "证明"/"proven" unless
  the ledger literally supports it.
- If a qualifier won't fit the hook, it may live in the body instead — but it
  **must still appear somewhere in the post.**

## Examples

Each example shows hook + cover copy + a few body lines + hashtags, grounded in a
ledger claim with its qualifier kept (in the hook or the body). Illustrative —
use real ledger content.

**Example 1 — mouse cancer result**

```
EN
Hook: A drug shrank tumors 23%🐭 (in mice, for now)
Cover copy: Promising — but it's mice
Body:
🧪 New compound cut tumor volume ~23% over 4 weeks
🐭 Tested in mice only — not yet in humans
⏳ Preliminary: early-stage result, not a treatment
Hashtags: #科研日常 #抗癌研究 #看论文 #健康科普

ZH
钩子：一种药让肿瘤缩小 23%🐭（目前是小鼠）
封面文案：有希望，但还是小鼠实验
正文：
🧪 新化合物 4 周内让肿瘤体积缩小约 23%
🐭 仅在小鼠中测试——尚未用于人体
⏳ 初步结果：早期发现，还不是治疗手段
标签：#科研日常 #抗癌研究 #看论文 #健康科普
```

**Example 2 — coffee / diabetes association**

```
EN
Hook: More coffee, lower diabetes risk?☕
Cover copy: A link — not proof
Body:
☕ Big study: more coffee → lower type-2 diabetes risk
🔗 It's a correlation, NOT proof coffee causes it
👀 Don't change your habits based on one study
Hashtags: #咖啡 #健康饮食 #科普 #糖尿病

ZH
钩子：咖啡喝得多，糖尿病风险低？☕
封面文案：是关联，不是证据
正文：
☕ 大型研究：咖啡越多，2 型糖尿病风险越低
🔗 这是相关，并不能证明是咖啡的功劳
👀 别只凭一项研究就改变习惯
标签：#咖啡 #健康饮食 #科普 #糖尿病
```

**Example 3 — preliminary brain-implant pilot**

```
EN
Hook: Typing with your thoughts?🧠 (1 person, early)
Cover copy: Real, but very early
Body:
🧠 Brain implant may have sped up typing for a paralyzed user
1️⃣ Based on a single participant — a pilot
⏳ Preliminary: needs much more testing
Hashtags: #脑机接口 #黑科技 #神经科学 #科普

ZH
钩子：用意念打字？🧠（仅 1 人，早期）
封面文案：真实，但非常早期
正文：
🧠 脑机接口或让一名瘫痪者打字更快
1️⃣ 仅基于一名受试者——属试验性质
⏳ 初步结果：还需大量验证
标签：#脑机接口 #黑科技 #神经科学 #科普
```

## Red lines

Follow [`../rules/red_lines.md`](../rules/red_lines.md): ledger-only, keep every
qualifier, never overstate. **The short format is NOT a license to drop
qualifiers** — if it won't fit the hook, put it in the body, but never omit it.
